  • IDP required — both drivers; get at AAA before the trip

Day trips & en-route stops

Granada · Guadix day (Oct 5)

  • Guadix — cave quarter, miradores, cathedral
  • Altiplano winery visit + long lunch (names, prices and booking on the Dining page)
  • La Calahorra Castle — exterior viewpoint (interior is Fri–Sun and Oct 5 is a Monday; the flip was dropped)

En route to Seville · Carmona (Oct 10)

  • Directly on the A-4, ~35 min short of Seville — near-free on a drive already being made
  • Necrópolis Romana — rock-cut tombs (Elefante, Servilia)
  • Alcázar de la Puerta de Sevilla — Carthaginian → Roman → Moorish in one gate-fortress
  • Long old-town lunch, then on to Seville late afternoon

Itálica dropped

  • Skipped. Itálica is closed Mondays, and Oct 12 — the only morning it could have taken — is a Monday
  • Sunday Oct 11 is the full Alcázar day and Itálica shuts at 15:00 on Sundays, so there was no way to fit it without damaging a better day
  • The Cathedral & Giralda keep the Oct 12 morning. Carmona carries the Roman layer on this leg, alongside Córdoba's bridge and temple

Ronda area

  • En route: Antequera dolmens & El Torcal (Oct 6)
  • Zahara de la Sierra (Oct 7 anchor) — Nasrid castle keep over a turquoise reservoir; scenic drive up the Puerto de las Palomas pass
  • Grazalema — pair with Zahara; village in the natural park
  • Setenil de las Bodegas — houses under the rock
  • Local wineries, Serranía de Ronda DO (which one, and how to book, on the Dining page)
